Welcome to Inclusion in Action

We all have a role to play in creating a workplace where everyone can thrive.

High‑Performing Teams in action

Small, intentional actions can make a meaningful difference:

  • Ask for feedback regularly, not just during formal reviews.
  • Recognize contributions and celebrate progress.
  • Invite diverse perspectives before making decisions.
  • Create spaces for everyone to participate and contribute.
  • Communicate with clarity and consistency through change.

Day 3 - 16 September

Accessibility

Designed for everyone. Built to adapt. We remove barriers and design inclusive experiences, today and for the future.

Visit us at the Booth
10–11am & 3–4pm
Question of the day
“Where could greater accessibility have the most impact?”

How do we design an inclusive workplace?

1 in 6people globally live with a disability
~80%of disabilities are non‑apparent

Designing accessibility from the start. Accessibility isn't something we add later; it's how we create better experiences for employees, clients, and Communities. At Bloomberg, we're strengthening accessibility through thoughtful workplace design, more consistent experiences, and a continued focus on removing barriers so everyone can contribute and perform at their best.

Accessibility in action

Small, intentional changes can make a meaningful difference:

  • Add alternative text to images and visual content.
  • Share materials in advance to support different ways of preparing.
  • Use accessible templates and formatting for documents.
  • Enable captions for presentations and virtual meetings.
  • Consider accessibility from the start when designing processes, products, and experiences.

Looking for more guidance? Explore the tools, resources and practical advice from our partners: Business Disability Forum and DisabilityIN.

Spotlight: Rhonda Calder‑Blissett Global Communities Lead

Since July 2025, Rhonda has served as Bloomberg's Global Inclusion Communities Lead. For Rhonda, Communities are about more than connection — they are a platform to strengthen culture, develop talent, and create meaningful business impact. Her focus is on helping Communities and their leaders thrive through clearer structures, stronger support, and opportunities for colleagues to build skills, expand networks, and grow as leaders.

This work is ultimately about collective ownership — creating space for different perspectives, meaningful allyship and ensuring voices are genuinely seen and heard.— RCB

Communities in action

Communities are one of the most visible ways employees bring Inclusion in Action to life. Across Bloomberg, they create opportunities to build relationships beyond day‑to‑day teams, share experiences, broaden perspectives, and develop leadership capabilities. Strong Communities are powered by passionate Co‑Leads, supportive managers, and Executive Sponsors who connect employee perspectives to broader business priorities.
