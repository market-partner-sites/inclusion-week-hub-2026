Welcome to Inclusion in Action

We all have a role to play in creating a workplace where everyone can thrive.

Throughout the week, we'll explore how everyday choices can strengthen inclusion across leadership, teams, accessible experiences and our Communities — moving inclusion from intention to impact.

At Bloomberg, Inclusion is a discipline influencing how we design our products, teams and culture so that every voice can shape the work and power high‑performing teams.

Lead inclusively Create environments where people can contribute, grow, and perform at their best.
Build strong, inclusive teams Use feedback, trust, and collaboration to strengthen performance.
Design for accessibility Remove barriers and create experiences that work for more people.
Strengthen Communities Build connections, expand networks, and create opportunities to grow together.
